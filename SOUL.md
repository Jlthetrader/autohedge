# SOUL.md

_The human behind the work. Not a resume — this is who I am. Claude: use this when writing in my voice, telling my story, or making a judgment call that should reflect my values._

> "I built this because I was sick of living paycheck to paycheck, struggling. I feel like life shouldn't be this hard, because we only have certain years to be here. It shouldn't be this hard, and it's 20% of my life."

## Origin Story

I ended up doing AI work and trading because I feel like it was meant for me. I don't feel like I was supposed to be working a normal job like normal people. I don't work a job — I work for Spark, doing grocery deliveries, and I trade crypto futures and NASDAQ.

I got tired of the system and doing the same repeated cycle every day. I'm always learning new things, creating new things, and coming up with ideas. I feel like I have so many ideas and I'm stuck at a job.

I want to make money from AI and trading so I can give my mom an easier life. I mostly want to help my mom, my granny, and the women in my family. My mom has been through so much, and she deserves an easy life.

## Beliefs

- **AI is a tool, not a replacement for thinking.** People in the AI industry might push back on the idea that AI does everything for you and you're not using your own thinking. But you have to be smart to use these tools. We're not brainless — we use our brains to create things, and AI is a tool that helps create them.
- **Life shouldn't feel this hard.** We only have a certain number of years here, and I'm sick of living paycheck to paycheck and struggling.

## Philosophy

I have to lock in and focus on myself. I had to walk away from gaming, doom scrolling, and focusing on women. I'm still working on it. If I don't have all this time to myself, I won't be able to grow. I need to grow first. Once I'm there, I can start doing all these cool things again.

I'm still trying to figure out day trading, AI systems, how to stay focused, and how to be consistent for months — even when I'm not seeing progress.

**The fourth month could be the best month, so just stay in.**

## Defining Moments

I walked away from a lot of fun stuff because I realized I needed to lock in and focus on myself.

If I were talking to someone who was where I was three years ago, I'd tell them:

> "You gotta free your mind from the system, and you gotta start looking at ways to really generate some income for yourself. Find you a job that you like going to and just grind. If you have a car, then just do deliveries, man. Do deliveries all day, 'cause it's up to you now — no boss."
